# 3D Border Inspector

## Ownership and contract

The Border Inspector separates **physical boundary ownership** from **view-only presentation**. It does not alter process geometry, model layers, saved Project/History, GLB exports, or Recovery transactions.

- **Exterior**: already-owned exposed material perimeter and genuine exposed step edges (`plan.borderLines`).
- **Material interfaces**: perimeters of owned horizontal material junction caps (`cap.buried` with a distinct `interfaceLayerId`).
- **Buried features**: remaining buried cap perimeters and real buried sidewall corners and upper/lower ends. Same-material contiguous Z spans are coalesced; collinear computational wall partitions do not create vertical lines.
- **Occluded edges**: existing exterior outlines that fail the closest *structural* depth comparison; available as dashed or solid reference lines, independent of physical classes.
- **ROI cut**: separate contour ownership for genuinely sliced/cut structural surfaces. It is never inferred from mesh triangles.

The first three categories are generated from the canonical owned surface plan (`three-outline-geometry.js`, `renderer-geometry.js`, `process-topology.js`). They must not be generated by edge-detecting tessellation, because triangle/LOD seams are not physical boundaries. The UI is in `ui-v2/three-view.js`; 3D presentation and depth classification are in `three-view.js` and `three-outline-pass.js`.

## Display policy

Each category accepts Off, Auto, Solid, or Dashed. At 100% material opacity, Auto hides internal material interfaces and buried features; below 100% it renders interfaces as softer solid lines and buried features as softer dashed lines. Explicit Solid/Dashed overrides Auto, including in opaque X-Ray inspection.

The Clean preset displays the exterior and ROI; Structure also enables both internal categories when transparent; X-Ray explicitly displays internal geometry and dashed occluded exterior reference edges. Border line strength is a view-local scalar. Line dashes are camera-scaled for legibility and rendered with Three `LineDashedMaterial` rather than a one-off mask effect.

Transparent interior contour lines are drawn in a dedicated no-depth-test inspection overlay, **after** translucent surface colors. Exterior solid lines still pass structural depth testing and occluded references use greater-depth classification. The overlay deliberately shows through material; its alpha is capped so it remains subordinate to the material rendering. Changing styles or line strength does not change the Process/Kernel model.

The settings are currently local to the running Workstation (except the existing persisted `threeShowBorders` master switch). Persisting the detailed style choices across browser reloads needs a separate explicit display-state storage contract and should not silently create History or Recovery mutations.

## Focused acceptance

1. For each class, test Off, Auto, Solid, Dashed and master Border OFF with 100%, 70%, 45%, and 15% material opacity on the same camera/ROI.
2. Verify true buried interfaces and Via edges without introducing computational seams, duplicate depth contours, mesh triangle edges, floating lines or artificial cracks. Verify cut-only outlines against rough/pyramid mesh boundaries and Section A–B.
3. Re-run native-v2 Chromium on small stacked films, photodetector, M3D, PERC and an array, inspecting screenshots at multiple zooms and edge-on angles. Measure frame time and memory for repeated preset changes.
4. Before merging, execute renderer/geometry targeted tests, Quality, and risk-relevant scientific/History/Recovery gates. Never silently relax tolerances or approve screenshots from a mocked UI.
