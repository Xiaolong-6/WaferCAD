### A critical result from independent geometry, not a screenshot impression

The rebuilt baseline `swx-sem-v2-unit.wafercad` at `9f026b6c` has **196 total source Mask polygons** (162 positive silicon / 28 shallow-etch / 6 electrode). Its unioned silicon contains **4 disconnected components**; the occupied silicon bounds are approximately **x=[−76,76] µm, y=[−103,−13.55] µm**, i.e. **152 × 89.45 µm**. A 160 × 260 µm sample process field is **not** the paper's 95 × 100 µm device footprint. Likewise neither the full-mask occupied bbox nor the bounding box of grating couplers can be treated as a like-for-like measurement of the paper's 95 × 100 µm mechanical cell.

The separate `swx-sem-ground-return-candidate.wafercad` supersedes the earlier two 6 µm BOX-supported rail experiment. It uses **assumed narrow Ground traces (0.8 µm) and Signal traces (1.2 µm)**, a **6 µm externally supported Signal bus**, and one additional BOX-supported right Ground anchor; eight metal contacts are modeled. It reduces separate Si electrical domains from **4 to 2**, joining the fixed optical structure to the Ground net while retaining an isolated stationary-comb Signal net. **None of those external route/pad coordinates is recovered from author GDS or Fig. 3a.** Passing a Kernel undercut check does **not** demonstrate paper-faithful release. This remains a **routing feasibility experiment, not a source-verified repair**.

A further independent audit of actual OFF/ON **post-HF BOX** geometry at `9f026b6c` found **56.588854 µm** of _continuous unsupported centerline path_ across the lower bends of each Ground trace, and **54.059939 / 53.059939 µm** on the Signal legs. The previously reported **35.905 µm** refers only to the longest unsupported _portion of an individual straight layout segment_; it understates the longer continuous unsupported path across a corner. These are path arc lengths, **not validated straight-column buckling lengths**. Neither source-measured film residual stress, boundary conditions nor a released-beam FEM is available. The scientific test now requires the corner-continuous BOX audit, and mechanical acceptance remains blocked.

The live independent results are retained in the [SEM-v2 exact-head workflow artifact](https://github.com/Xiaolong-6/WaferCAD/actions/runs/39026916427) and the `sourceElectricalNetAudit` sections of the original/candidate `science-report.json` files.

The retained BOX underneath the experimentally routed **122 µm external Signal bus** and the unverified right-hand Ground anchor must not be hidden under the old report's hardcoded `extraneousSourceUnverifiedBOXSupportedRails: 0`. The revised actual-post-HF audit records **two examined, source-unverified retained-BOX features** for OFF and ON: Signal bus (122 µm supported centerline) and right Ground anchor (approximately 4.126708 µm). This is deliberately a **non-exhaustive** inventory: it does not certify every other Si/BOX interface, pad or trench. The author-layout and mechanical acceptance flags remain false.

### 2026-10-19: Original SEM Fig. 3a visual rejection of the fabricated four-sided perimeter

Direct review of the original article **Fig. 3a SEM**, compared with the paper's Fig. 1c schematic, Fig. 4b switch-cell micrograph and Supplementary S6–S9, rejects the previous **four-sided 200 × 280 µm bulk-Si rectangular ring with a 164 × 113 µm closed cavity**. That outline is not demonstrated in any of those images, and its continuous top/bottom BOX-backed wafer slabs visually dwarf the reported **95 × 100 µm** switching cell. Those figures are neither author-mask coordinates nor reliable Si/BOX depth segmentation.

A subsequent real Chromium comparison also rejected the experimental **two disconnected open context flanks**: they still appeared as unsupported bulk-Si slabs in the actual 3D view. The current SEM comparison study therefore adds **zero speculative peripheral Si polygons** and retains the separately experimental Ground/Signal Mask, fixed-comb supports and suspended core. The 200 × 280 µm processing field is only a study canvas, **not** the article's 95 × 100 µm measured switch footprint. The `sem-context` camera provides a closer view of the actual device rather than allowing the empty study field to dominate the frame. This is a correction of ungrounded geometry, not a claim of full source reproduction. No outer contour or BOX ownership should be promoted without registered source evidence; original author GDS, release-front metrology and mechanical FEM remain unavailable.

**2026-10-19 SEM material-tracing experiment (not paper acceptance).** Registered the provided Fig. 3a SEM crop to the existing switch Mask using its 15 µm scale-bar region and anchor/comb landmarks, then traced medium-grey contiguous features into a restricted experimental positive-Si candidate. 115 simplified, hole-free sample contours cover approximately 1138 µm² of _additional proposed Si_, after a conservative 1.8 µm guard around pre-existing OFF-mask silicon. The export uses polygon strips instead of a guessed closed rectangular frame; original Mask/actuator/optics remain unchanged. The selection of grey thresholds and pixel registration is **approximate**, SEM contrast does **not** establish material composition or BOX continuity, and the added polygons are **not author GDS**. Independent scientific guards must check no electrical short to the existing Signal/Ground network, actual post-HF mesa material and continued suspension of the center SWX. The previous zero-perimeter experiment is superseded as a visual comparison, not promoted as source-verified.

## Immediate P0 decisions

1. **Keep both geometry variants experimental.** Do not register either as the official Example or silently use the two rails as the paper's peripheral mask. The 64 × 64 physical array stays stopped.
2. **Reconcile source coordinate registration first**: pick one consistent physical orientation from Fig. 1c and 3a, identify the two fixed optical feeds, the moving leads, anchor/pad footprints, four spring attachments, comb handles and real isolation trenches. The 95 × 100 µm frame refers to the switch body, **not** the 160 × 260 µm illustration/process field. Resolve missing outer context and ground path together, not independently.
3. **Rebuild real Si and BOX ownership as one topology**. The stationary optical structure must connect to grounded Si without shorting a stationary drive and without retaining large, unverified BOX-backed peripheral structures. Every supported island must be identified as an anchor or stationary comb; every moving/suspended beam must have a release contract.
4. **Visual acceptance before broad CI**: generate enlarged **actual WaferCAD Chromium** OFF/ON Mask, SWX/comb/spring 3D, and multiple Section transects through supported fixed comb, suspended hairpins/shuttle, and the perimeter/ground connection. Compare against paper Figure 3a and Figure 1c, documenting unknown dimensions rather than guessing.
5. **Then** independent exact-GDS/OAS parity, Recipe/History/Recovery science invariants, Main integration conflict audit, final Quality, and review. Main merge requires user approval.

## Source confidence policy

- **Paper-reported:** film/material/process thicknesses, 900 nm/15 nm OFF/ON gaps, SWT, optical/total switch footprint, stationary-versus-movable supports, spring/comb principle.
- **Inferred in current candidate:** XY terminal and metal pad coordinates, two 6 µm external ground rails, comb frame registration, S7/S6 terminal attachment positions, illustrative fillet contours and HF lateral reach.
- **Unverified/not claimed:** original EBL GDS, nanometer sidewall taper, calibrated HF kinetics and full release boundary, coupled FDTD/FEM, source-author exact perimeter Si/isolation geometry.

**Scientific acceptance is an explicit FAIL/BLOCKED state whenever grounding, source dimensional registration, suspension ownership or SEM perimeter structure is unresolved — regardless of workflow green status.**
