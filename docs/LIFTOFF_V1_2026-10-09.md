## Modeling limits

No solvent diffusion, adhesion, tearing, mechanical fracture, pinch-off or arbitrary 3D connected-component resolution. A continuous film over an aperture may have different real-world lift-off behavior; this operator rejects geometrically ambiguous bridges rather than deciding mechanically. Neither the PMMA removal nor the Cr mask implies a simulated experimental RIE rate.
