## Implementation

- Independent Lift-off in the Step UI, with sacrificial layer, processing face
  and area / ROI selection.
- Declarative `liftoff({ sacrificial: "PMMA", face: "front", area: "full" })`
  command, Recipe editor, preflight, History recording / replay, and
  sacrificial-layer ID remapping after upstream changes.
- Ideal 2.5D support semantics: remove one sacrificial material interval and
  the outward, directly supported stack within each XY column. Cr deposited
  at a lower physical Z in patterned resist openings remains.
- Uncertain continuous film bridges fail closed. A partial-area operation
  also considers films left outside the selected ROI, so cutting a supported
  film at the ROI boundary cannot silently succeed.
- Support canonical array templates and safe no-op behavior in cells with
  no sacrificial material. Keep the worker candidate transaction, project
  validator, export, and recovery contracts intact.
- No new project schema version. The Recipe command whitelist, process guide,
  and Wiki were updated.

## Modeling and acceptance boundaries

The result models ideal geometry. It does not predict solvent dissolution,
adhesion, metal tearing, mechanical bridge fracture, sidewall pinch-off,
or process kinetics. Same-layer overlapping-Z regions that could form a
continuous film are conservatively rejected, even when a real patterned
deposit might be mechanically separable.

Before declaring product UX acceptance or merging #167:

1. Review the Lift-off Step and Recipe controls in the actual application at
   wide and narrow viewports; check selected targets and failure feedback.
2. Check recovery from a browser reload and history edits in an interactive
   session; automated Recipe and export/reimport tests have already passed.
3. Benchmark the nonuniform, approximately 4,725-feature Metalens when an
   original or paper-derived layout is prepared. The 625-site periodic test
   is not representative of unique nanopillar geometries.
4. Confirm all required CI checks are green at the exact final head. Do not
   update visual baselines merely to hide regressions.

No force pushes or manual expensive CI triggers were used.
